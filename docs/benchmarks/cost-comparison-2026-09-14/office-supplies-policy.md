# Office Supplies Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Purchases

Employees may purchase standard office supplies — pens, notebooks, printer
paper, and desk organizers — up to $40 per month without prior approval.
Purchases above $40 in a single month require written approval from a
direct manager before the purchase is made, not after.

## Equipment

Requests for equipment (monitors, keyboards, headsets, chairs) are handled
separately from the monthly supplies allowance and must go through the IT
procurement form, regardless of cost.

## Reimbursement Method

Approved supply purchases are reimbursed via the next scheduled payroll
run, not as a standalone payment. Receipts must be submitted within 14
days of purchase; purchases submitted after 14 days are not reimbursed.

## Restricted Items

Personal subscriptions, furniture beyond a standard desk chair, and any
item costing more than $150 individually are never eligible under this
policy, even with manager approval.
